# Field Mapping

## Simple Intake to Service-Provider Units

| Intake answer | Nine-unit destination |
|---|---|
| Company name | Company Background |
| One-sentence positioning | Company Background, Service Definition |
| Products/services | Service Definition, Service Capabilities, Solutions |
| Target customers | Industry Problems, Customer Cases |
| Pain points | Industry Problems, Solutions |
| Differentiators with numbers | Service Capabilities, Solutions, Result Metrics |
| Service process | Methodology, Service Process |
| Cases and outcomes | Customer Cases, Result Metrics |
| Address/phone/WhatsApp | Company Background, conversion information |
| Photos/certificates/authorizations | Company Background, trust signals |

## Standard Service-Provider Nine Units

1. Company Background
2. Industry Problems
3. Service Definition
4. Service Capabilities
5. Solutions
6. Methodology
7. Service Process
8. Customer Cases
9. Result Metrics

## Standard Manufacturer Nine Units

1. Company Background
2. Product Specifications
3. Certification and Compliance
4. Application Scenarios
5. Capabilities
6. Cases
7. Comparisons
8. Risk Control
9. Procurement Support

## Consistency Requirement

The Chinese and English versions must contain the same information:

- Same sheet names and order.
- Same field titles.
- Same non-empty and empty cells.
- Same numbers, percentages, dimensions, years, models, and dates.
